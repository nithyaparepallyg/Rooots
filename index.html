Wait, why do we do that?

Every tradition began as someone’s idea — a clever fix, a way to mark a moment, a story worth keeping. We want to understand that thinking, so you can step inside a tradition instead of admiring it from the outside.

First, we learn and credit where a tradition comes from. Our version sits beside the original — never in its place.
