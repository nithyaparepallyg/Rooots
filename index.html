STORIESMAKING

We bring cultures to life through stories, discoveries and making.

Every experience invites children and families to discover a tradition, decode the ideas behind it and reinterpret what they've learned through their own imagination.

Our vision

We want people of every age to understand the roots of culture, and feel free to grow something new from them.

Too often, culture is memorised or admired from a distance. We believe it stays alive when people get close to it: when they look closely, ask why, understand the ideas and people behind it, and then make it their own. Rooots creates that space for children, families and elders, together.

  • People who ask why.
  • Generations who make together.
  • Traditions that keep growing.

Our way
