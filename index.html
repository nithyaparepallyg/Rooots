What inspires us

We’re drawn to the things people have done for generations, and the reasons behind them.

Festivals and rituals. Folk tales and the stories grandparents tell. Patterns on cloth, indigenous tattoos, crafts passed from hand to hand. Even small everyday habits, like how a meal is served or a doorstep is decorated.

Most of these are so familiar we stop noticing them. But each one began as someone’s idea: a way of solving a problem, marking a moment or telling a story. That’s the part we’re curious about. Once you see the thinking behind a tradition, it’s no longer just something to admire from a distance. It becomes something you can talk back to.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Observe

    We start by looking closely at something familiar, as if seeing it for the first time.

  2. 02

    Question

    Why this shape, this colour, this story? We ask the obvious questions, and the odd ones too.

  3. 03

    Interpret

    We find the idea behind it and think about what it could mean today.

  4. 04

    Make

    Then we reinterpret it in our own way, and leave room for others to find theirs.
